RICOCHET Privacy Policy

Pedro's Awesome Gaming Studio develops RICOCHET.

Data collection and sharing

RICOCHET does not collect or share personal data through the game. It has no ads, player accounts, or in-app purchases. Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, and Unity's player data collection services are disabled for RICOCHET.

Game data stored on your device

RICOCHET stores saved games, high scores, in-game coins, cosmetic unlocks and selections, and settings on your device so you can keep your progress and preferences. The game does not send these data to the developer or to Unity. The data remain on your device until you clear them.

Android backups and diagnostics

Depending on your device settings, Android Auto Backup may include RICOCHET's local data in backups managed by Google and restore them later. Android and Google Play may independently process technical diagnostics, such as crashes and app performance, under your device settings and Google's policies. Google Privacy Policy

Deleting your local game data

To delete your local data, open SETTINGS in RICOCHET and choose NUKE ALL, or open Android Settings, select RICOCHET, and clear the app's storage. Manage any existing Android backups separately in your device's backup settings.
